MISTBROOK · CASE BRIEF

Pinewood Inn

You are a detective, called to the quiet town of Mistbrook to investigate a mysterious death at the Pinewood Inn.

Last night, a guest named Jake died in an upstairs room. Three witnesses heard sounds coming from the room around the time of his death.

All three are speaking honestly. Yet each has a different explanation for one of the voices.

Your investigation begins at the hotel entrance. The receptionist is expecting you. Guests and staff continue their daily routines. Somewhere inside, a recording of that night is waiting to be found.

Explore the rooms. Talk to people. Listen to what happens in the hallways. Replay anything you do not understand. A passing conversation may change how you interpret an earlier clue.

Your task is to identify the sources of those sounds, explain the conflicting accounts, and reconstruct what happened around Jake’s death.
